FREQUENTLY ASKED

Common Questions from Managing Partners

Does CAFlow support both Bikram Sambat (BS) and Gregorian (AD) dates?

Yes! CAFlow is engineered specifically for Nepal's fiscal calendar (e.g. 2081/82) and provides automatic conversion between BS deadlines and AD timestamps.

Can article trainees access all firm clients?

No. CAFlow enforces role-based access control. Article trainees only see clients and working papers explicitly assigned to their engagement team.

How secure is client data stored in CAFlow?

All client files and PAN records are encrypted with AES-256. PostgreSQL data isolation guarantees your firm's data is never visible to other firms.
